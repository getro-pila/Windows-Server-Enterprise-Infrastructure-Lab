# Source material and limitations

Sources reviewed:

- `Fall 2025 NOS 145 Final Exam Scenario(1).docx` — scenario and grading requirements
- `Fall 2025 NOS 145 Final Exam Screenshots Worksheet (1).docx` — populated screenshot worksheet (primary evidence)
- `Fall 2025 NOS 145 Final Exam Screenshots Worksheet(2).docx` — mostly blank screenshot template

The scenario and blank worksheet are not uploaded in full, to avoid presenting assignment instructions as original work. Screenshots are extracted from the populated worksheet in their original embedded resolution. `evidence-XX` numbers follow image order in that document (excluding its introductory example image), rather than necessarily matching graded step numbers. Category folders are approximate navigation aids; confirm a screenshot's content before citing it as proof of a specific step.

No passwords or fabricated configurations were added. The diagram and subnet plan are documentation derived from the assignment design.
